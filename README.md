# Leonida Ledger

An offline GTA VI PS5 trophy field guide and progress tracker for all 55 trophies. It includes a source-cited guide beside every official name, description and grade, a staged platinum roadmap, missable and chapter-lock flags, local notes, spoiler-aware story entries, and progress export/import. Checklist progress and notes stay in this browser's local storage; the app makes no runtime network calls.

Open `app/index.html` from a static server, or run `node serve.ts` and visit `http://localhost:8080`. The entrypoint `node main.ts` copies the static app and screenshots to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and builds the competition dossier.

## Source and uncertainty notes

The PowerPyx GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap and its 100% Completion Checklist were read while building this guide. Trophy methods are paraphrased and cited in each entry. PowerPyx reports the Chapter 1 home meditation as potentially missable but says whether it is required is unconfirmed; it also leaves Fair Game's exact animal trigger unconfirmed. The competition starter gives the aggregate total of 28 PlayStation-hidden trophies but no per-trophy hidden markers, so spoiler mode masks all unearned story trophies rather than inventing individual hidden flags.
